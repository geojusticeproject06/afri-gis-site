GOVERNMENT PLANNING

How can we better understand where infrastructure investment is most needed?

Afri-GIS overlays population, income, road networks, schools, healthcare, utilities, and economic activity to produce a unified picture.

WHAT THE PICTURE REVEALS
  • Infrastructure gaps — where critical services and connectivity are absent.
  • Underserved communities — populations lacking access to essential resources.
  • Priority investment areas — supporting capital planning and grant applications.
  • Development opportunities — high-potential zones for public and private investment.
